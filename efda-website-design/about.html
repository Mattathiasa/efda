(01) Who we are

Education as an instrument of change — in everything we do.

Education For Development Association (EFDA) is an Ethiopian civil society organization working through community-based development in Oromia and Benishangul Gumuz.

Alongside standalone basic education projects run since our founding, EFDA uses education as an instrument of change in every other program — environmental protection, livelihoods, community health and peacebuilding — so that knowledge stays in the community long after a project ends.

Our education work spans Alternative Basic Education, quality improvement inside government primary schools, and the Speed School programme for children out of school for socioeconomic reasons.

(02) Development philosophy

Vision

A literate, self-reliant and productive community that is capable of solving its economic, social and environmental problems.

Mission

To facilitate, foster and influence a process of ongoing change in favour of the poor through community-based development.

Overall goal

To empower communities to manage their own economic, social, environmental and health problems — relying on their own skills, knowledge and resources, with minimum dependence on external support.

(03) Organization

How EFDA is organized.

A Board of Directors governs EFDA, alongside an independent external auditor. The Managing Director leads two arms: Program Management, which runs our project offices, and Finance & Administration.
