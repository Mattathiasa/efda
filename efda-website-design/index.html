(01) What we stand for

We measure our work by how little a community needs us afterwards — its own skills, knowledge and resources doing the work.

Vision

01

A literate, self-reliant and productive community that is capable of solving its economic, social and environmental problems.

Mission

02

To facilitate, foster and influence a process of ongoing change in favour of the poor through community-based development.

Overall goal

03

To empower communities to manage their own economic, social, environmental and health problems — relying on their own skills, knowledge and resources, with minimum dependence on external support.

(02) One integrated approach

Five programs.One community.

A child who can read, a hillside that holds its soil, a hive that earns, a healthy mother and a village at peace are parts of the same life. EFDA works on them together, through the community’s own institutions.

(03) Our story

From the shade of a tree to 181 schools.

In 2000, EFDA’s first Alternative Basic Education classes met outdoors, under trees. Since then, communities, government and EFDA have built and equipped classrooms, libraries and furniture — and education has become the thread running through every other project we run.
